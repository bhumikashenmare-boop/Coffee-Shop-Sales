## ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Business Intelligence Case Study

## Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data to uncover customer purchasing patterns, revenue drivers, product performance, and store-level trends. Using transactional sales data, the analysis transforms raw business records into actionable insights that can support decision-making, operational efficiency, and revenue growth.

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project was to identify key factors influencing sales performance and provide data-driven recommendations to improve business outcomes across multiple store locations.

## Key Insights

* Identified peak sales hours and customer purchasing behavior.
* Analyzed revenue trends across different months.
* Evaluated store-wise sales performance.
* Determined top-performing products by quantity sold and revenue generated.
* Assessed product category contributions to overall revenue.
* Calculated key business KPIs such as Total Revenue and Average Order Value (AOV).

## Key Findings

* Morning hours (8 AM – 10 AM) generated the highest sales activity.
* Revenue showed strong month-over-month growth, with June recording the highest sales.
* Hell's Kitchen emerged as the top-performing store location.
* Barista Espresso generated the highest revenue among all products.
* Coffee and Tea categories contributed the majority of total sales revenue.

## Business Recommendations

* Strengthen breakfast and morning promotional campaigns.
* Introduce weekend-focused marketing initiatives.
* Expand high-performing espresso-based product offerings.
* Implement customer loyalty programs to increase repeat purchases.
* Leverage top-selling products for cross-selling opportunities.

## Outcome

This project demonstrates how data analytics can be used to convert raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and strategic recommendations, showcasing practical skills relevant to Data Analyst and Business Analyst roles.
